A little history

RecognizeGood

It started with a thank-you that didn't feel like enough.

RecognizeGood was founded in Austin around 2009, born from a simple belief: that the good people do deserves to be seen. What began as a humble "Thank You Wall" grew into a foundation devoted to illuminating everyday kindness — through its Legend and Lighthouse Awards, its pay-it-forward giving, and its lasting mission to elevate the spirit of community by recognizing acts of good.

The 25 people and groups on this wall are among those RecognizeGood has honored — neighbors who showed up, again and again, for others.

Kudos

A thank-you made permanent.

Kudos is a medium for sharing kindness on the Solana blockchain, created to provide recognition that lasts FOREVER. Each one is non-tradeable and carries no price — its only value is what it means.

Because it lives on-chain, a Kudos can't be edited, sold, or erased. It's a way to say "this mattered" that endures. Here, each Kudos is paired with a real act of kindness — a permanent record beside a human story.

Together, they stand for the same idea from two directions: that kindness is worth noticing, worth honoring, and worth keeping. RecognizeGood tells the story; Kudos makes it last.
