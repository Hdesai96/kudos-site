What happens after you hit send

No money changes hands. No one buys anything. Here's the whole path from your nomination to a permanent record.

1

Every nomination is read by a real person, not a bot or an algorithm.
2
The community weighs in
Members confirm the act reflects what KUDS stands for: genuine, generous, human.
3
One KUDS is awarded
A single token transfers to the recipient's Solana wallet. No cost to them, ever.
