How it works

From a kind act to a permanent record.

No applications, no purchases, no points to grind. Recognition starts with someone else noticing what you did.

STEP 1
An act of kindness
Someone helps, mentors, supports, or quietly fixes the thing so others won't trip on it.
STEP 2
A peer nominates
Another community member notices and puts the act forward for recognition.
STEP 3
The community agrees
Members confirm it reflects the spirit of KUDS — genuine, generous, human.
STEP 4
Written to stay
One KUDS transfers to their wallet. The record lives on Solana for as long as the network does.

KUDS is a non-financial social recognition token. It is not a security, investment product, or financial instrument of any kind. It carries no monetary value by design, pays no returns, and cannot be bought, sold, traded, or redeemed for currency, goods, or services through any mechanism endorsed by the Kudos Project. It exists solely to acknowledge and celebrate positive contributions within communities.
